# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Photo contests that live in a discussion: started from the composer or from an existing discussion.
- Phases on a schedule: opening soon, taking entries, voting, decided.
- Entry limits per member; who may enter and vote by group; minimum account age and posts for voters; optional approval of entries.
- Community voting (vote limit, never your own, anonymous or named, live counts optional) or judges scoring 1–10.
- Photographers' names hidden until voting ends (optional), and a per-voter shuffled gallery.
- Gallery, lightbox with keyboard, swipe, full screen and slideshow; camera settings shown, location never stored.
- Winners' podium, alerts and email to winners, alerts for entrants when voting opens and results are in.
- FoF Badges triggers: contests won, podium finishes, photos entered.
- Vote review for staff with basic vote-ring detection; void and restore votes.
- Contests page at /contests.
- Uploads sniffed by content and re-encoded with all metadata removed; size and pixel caps; no SVG.
